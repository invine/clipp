# Visual review of the implemented Relay portal

Reviewed Relay `020f47da263d0765bae42b7505c760d8caac1a35` on `codex/relay-clipp-style-integration` against the [Clipp Relay mockup](../../mockups/clipp-relay/index.html). The linked implementation thread reported that browser-rendered visual acceptance had not been completed; it later created a disposable signed-out preview and recorded a user report of unreadable light-theme text, without identifying the affected page.

For this review, the exact revision's Go `portal.Render`, embedded templates, CSS/JS and histogram helper were copied to an isolated temporary module and rendered with synthetic owner/admin data. Chromium intercepted requests locally and blocked all external requests; no real account, connection, Clip, live provider, form submission or system clipboard was used. This is an actual template/asset render, **not** the production HTTP handler or a built-service acceptance test. Source and selected screenshots support these findings; unrendered states remain unverified.

## Findings, ordered by impact

1. **Dark-theme controls are nearly invisible.** In the [desktop account](evidence/implementation-desktop-account-dark.png), Light theme and sidebar Log out are dark text on dark backgrounds. The [Connect Clipp dialog](evidence/implementation-desktop-connect-dark.png) also has an almost invisible Close control. Computed text is `rgb(33, 26, 44)` on `rgb(23, 21, 29)` for the theme control, approximately 1.1:1 as reported by the app inspector; the same dark color is applied to the transparent logout and dialog-close controls. The broad dark button rule overrides their intended foreground. Fix all affected visible control states, including hover/focus and standalone pages, while retaining appropriate contrast for purple filled buttons.
2. **Mobile navigation is clipped despite a zero page-overflow measurement.** At 390 × 844, the Menu control's right edge measures x=413, 23 px beyond the viewport, inside the clipped workspace container; the [phone account](evidence/implementation-phone-account-dark.png) and [accounts](evidence/implementation-phone-accounts-dark.png) captures show its label cut off. `scrollWidth` still reports 390, so a no-horizontal-overflow check alone misses it. The top bar also squeezes the section title into two or three lines. Use the mockup's compact header and a fully visible menu/theme control; verify bounds and operability at 320–390 px, text zoom and long labels.
3. **Account overview duplicates and over-expands information.** The implementation's [desktop overview](evidence/implementation-desktop-account-dark.png) uses equal-height 451 px allowance and capacity cards, includes a large ring plus separate amount, repeats the session limit and expands the UTC explanation. The capacity card consequently has a large empty lower half and the history chart starts around y=770. The [reference](evidence/reference-desktop-account-dark.jpg) uses a compact allowance strip, succinct capacity rows and brings the recent usage chart into the first 900 px. At 390 px the allowance alone is 422 px and capacity is 331 px, putting the history section below the initial viewport. Recompose using the reference hierarchy without deleting quota-window rules, the independent session/grant explanations, or trustworthy data states.
4. **History becomes difficult to read on phones.** In the [phone account](evidence/implementation-phone-account-dark.png) the chart is hidden and the remaining three-column table forces “Observation” and “incomplete” to break within words; exact byte totals wrap through several lines. The reference shows a compact four-week graphic, with the current week visible. Preserve the complete 13-week range, exact values, zero/missing states, current-week identification and accessible table; reflow into readable rows or an accessible compact visual/list at phone widths without sideways page scrolling.
5. **The admin list gives half its desktop area to an empty editor.** The [desktop accounts view](evidence/implementation-desktop-accounts-dark.png) compresses the account table into a half-width card, splitting email addresses even at 1440 px while an unselected Review account pane holds only instructions. The mockup's [reference](evidence/reference-desktop-accounts-dark.png) makes the list full width and opens details on demand. Make selected review contextual or otherwise reclaim the empty pane; preserve the full real forms, status filters, bounded server-side search/paging, list context and no-script fallback. The synthetic [review capture](evidence/implementation-desktop-review-dark.png) is a pending sample and does not verify mutation behavior.
6. **Plan and security screens are much more vertically expansive than the reference.** The [phone plan view](evidence/implementation-phone-plans-dark.png) repeats field labels within each plan, places archive confirmation beside every record and pushes the next plan and create form far down; the [desktop security view](evidence/implementation-desktop-security-dark.png) gives full-width separate cards to related actions. The mockup uses compact rows and contextual detail. Align information and move optional detail/review into accessible task surfaces where suitable, but never remove create/archive forms, explicit confirmation, consequences or their safe no-script paths.
7. **Brand and surface language differ from the mockup.** The portal uses an abstract overlapping-squares mark, a purple-tinted inset workspace shell and outlined cards, while the reference uses Clipp's selected purple C mark, a flat near-black shell, neutral selected navigation and mostly tone-separated surfaces. Review [implementation](evidence/implementation-desktop-account-dark.png) versus [reference](evidence/reference-desktop-account-dark.jpg). Use the approved visual direction without changing an installed application icon; ensure any portal asset obeys existing embedded delivery and CSP.

## Inspector and limitations

The app inspector on the synthetic account render at 1440/768/390 reported three FAIL categories: purple palette, contrast, and edge text. Purple is explicitly part of the user-approved brand/reference and is not a defect; the contrast issue is confirmed above. The edge-text finding understates the measured mobile clipping. Source lint flags 40 patterns and 12 warnings, including app-appropriate table/form lines, IDs and overlay styles; these are diagnostics, not proof each declaration is visible. The synthetic fixtures do not exercise authentication, server policy, focus restoration across real requests, mutation results, all statuses, live/browser service behavior, or the whole state matrix. Inspect the actual built service with isolated fixtures before asserting complete coverage. The user's reported light-theme problem remains unlocated; the sampled light account render was legible, which does not establish that every light state is correct.

## App-screen critique

ROUND 1 — review only.

Seen in one second: a framed portal with two large account cards and controls that disappear into dark chrome.
Idea: an account owner should understand remaining weekly relay capacity and the separate limits on Relay Sessions and Login Grants at a glance.
Live thing: inspect recent Recorded Relay Usage and open Connect Clipp setup.
Layout words by view: account=ring and stretched cards / history=chart plus table / admin=split list and empty review / plans=stacked forms / security=large action cards.
Scores (subjective app-screen review): design 5.5 · usability 4.5 · creativity 6 · content 7 → overall 5.5. Cap: visible inspector contrast failures and clipped mobile control; marketing-page imagery rules are not applicable to this working app.
Top three problems:
1. Near-invisible theme, logout and dialog-close controls in dark mode; restore readable foreground in each state.
2. Mobile header clips Menu and wraps its title; reflow the compact shell and test actual control bounds.
3. Account and admin layouts stretch information and use half-width empty panes; adopt compact reference hierarchy without removing real forms or history.
