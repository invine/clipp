# Clipp UX study handoff

**Study complete — 2026-10-10.** Resolved in [Prioritize the UX findings and handoff](issues/08-prioritize-ux-study-handoff.md#answer). After the targeted native observations, the user accepted carrying the [exact remaining gaps](remaining-native-coverage.md) into implementation validation as unverified. The visual/control choices and correctness → compact workspace → additions are approved. This handoff contains the assessment, accepted direction and validation requirements; no production fixes or release qualification were performed.

The study identifies **51 runtime findings: 17 Electron, 16 Chrome and 18 Android**, plus source-supported control gaps. The strongest problems concern unreliable state/feedback, inaccessible controls and layouts that lose useful space at small sizes or enlarged text. The approved direction addresses those problems with shared styling and terminology, adapted layouts, and compact daily use.

## Design and behavior already settled

The [map's resolved decisions](map.md#decisions-so-far) are the decision index. Use these two contracts when implementing; older observation proposals and comparison variants are historical, not competing requirements:

- [Responsive layout and recovery contract](layout-state-contract.md): Clipp/status top line above Search; compact Filters/Send; All/Pinned only in Filters; narrow rows and uniform wide cards; no permanent sidebar; icon actions with names, focus and adequate hit targets; full-screen narrow tasks and comfortable desktop dialogs; session drafts on Back/Escape/Close; explicit Cancel/Discard; compact problem summary with task-local errors.
- [Control-flow contract](control-flow-contract.md): offline advanced identity reset, normal consequence confirmation without typed RESET, durable acceptance/retry and old public-ID receipt; Chrome popup quick actions with details/Delete and management in full-page; confirmation for Clear all only; current relay account actions; routine control cleanup.

Devices retain **text statuses**. The History header uses the selected S2 family: original green triangle for direct connections, amber for relayed connections or attention, gray for offline/waiting/unknown. A configured relay problem may require attention even while a direct route works. An ordinary offline Trusted Device or an intentionally empty relay configuration is not itself a fault. Auto Sync is separate; no icon or successful local action is a delivery receipt.

Preserve domain distinctions: Device Name versus Local Device Alias versus Peer ID; Trusted Device membership versus connection; local retention versus Clip Sharing Lifetime; Copy creates a new Local Clip while Send performs Share Now on the current clipboard; dismissing a Trust Request view does not invalidate the signed request. Reset does not erase the system clipboard or promise forensic erasure, other-device history deletion or network-wide revocation. Do not introduce new glossary terms or protocol semantics for presentation fixes.

The [prototype](layout-prototype/README.md) is a retained design comparison. The accepted full-screen task hierarchy, universal draft retention and consolidated issue summary are **not fully implemented in it**. Its historical toolbar counts before branding was restored must not be presented as the final header's density. Browser simulation, font scaling and token calculations are not native acceptance or accessibility certification.

## Ownership and completion checks
