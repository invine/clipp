**Parent:** [Approved specification](../spec.md).

- [ ] Coordinate adoption with the active UX owner. Reconcile current Git, candidate intent, claims, artifacts and applicable authorization; preserve unrelated dirty changes and historical results before migration.
- [ ] Apply the reviewed scenario/graph allocation and compact-state migration atomically enough that the tracker, catalog, traceability, coverage and status report agree. Every original criterion retains an owner; no unrun product gate becomes passed through migration.
- [ ] Adopt shared prerequisite code through the approved main-first integration policy. Preserve the existing UX branch and use an explicit reviewed adoption mapping; do not stack unrelated integration branches or copy unreviewed dirty source.
- [ ] A fresh coordinator starts at the canonical entry point and identifies the intended candidate, missing scenarios, resource owner, applicable authorization and next eligible action without reading archived conversations.
- [ ] Use the maintained Electron counterpart and either the qualified Android or Chrome path to run an actual Pairing scenario on a normally permitted route. Demonstrate that finite preparation reaches the product journey with candidate-bound observations, bounded diagnosis and cleanup.
- [ ] Record the actual journey result without conflating readiness, protocol-peer fixtures or one observed Pairing case with the complete rejection/acceptance/restart/exchange matrix. A product failure retains its regression and next action; an unavailable required native demonstration remains an unresolved qualification rather than a fixture-based pass.
- [ ] Demonstrate one legitimately completed scoped output being resolved and the next eligible work selected while an independent environment-blocked qualification remains visible. Existing claims can close only on their own satisfied requirements or explicitly recorded scoped user acceptance.
- [ ] Demonstrate unaffected evidence surviving a status/documentation-only successor and an affected scenario being withheld after a relevant change. Preserve original observations and applicability rationale.
- [ ] Run final applicable integration verification and Standards/Spec review, fixing blocking findings without restarting settled optional cleanup. Preserve evidence outside disposable worktrees and retire only this effort's eligible owned worktrees after integration/evidence checks.
- [ ] Deliver a compact continuation for every remaining UX scope with candidate, owner, exact missing scenario, blocker and next executable action. Report measured results, administrative acceptance, failures and not-run cases separately.

**Scope boundary:** Completion demonstrates adoption of all six improvements, not completion of the entire UX product backlog or permission to activate unqualified reset behavior. Publication, deployment, new account access and unrelated profile/worktree cleanup remain outside this ticket.
