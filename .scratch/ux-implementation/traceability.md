## How closure is collected

Each runtime finding has exactly one primary collector below. Supporting tickets provide implementation portions or evidence; they do not become additional owners of that finding. A ticket may finish its bounded implementation while a finding it collects remains open for another contribution or explicit native qualification. Conversely, a later presentation migration must retain an already verified early repair. Record implementation-ticket completion, finding closure and outstanding external qualification separately.

The supporting links are **not additional dependency edges**. Use individual tickets’ Blocked by fields, indexed in the catalog, for execution prerequisites; do not manufacture cycles by making an early collector wait for a later consumer before its own implementation can finish. For example, the shared-assets ticket can finish its foundation while C15 awaits all final surfaces; navigation can finish its shared presentation while E06/C06 await native input; basic reflow need not wait for final History density. Missing native evidence remains unqualified, never a fixture-derived pass. Where a row names a later migration that must preserve an already repaired behavior, that link records a regression obligation, not a reason to delay the early finding result. Broad integration findings such as C15 explicitly collect all final surfaces.

The allocation is **27 workspace-primary, 15 correctness-primary and 9 native-primary findings**, covering all 51 once. Original finding priorities remain **36 band 1, 13 band 2 and 2 band 3**. Ticket bands describe delivery: a first-band foundation may collect a later-band integration finding without accelerating every contributing package. Alias clearing and lifetime wiring may accompany their owners as approved; the advanced reset remains additional-controls work.

Ticket 32 is a supporting delivery refinement of the original assets/control package. Ticket 17 retains its original primary collector assignments and the required desktop qualification. The refinement closes no finding; compatible source is not desktop qualification.
