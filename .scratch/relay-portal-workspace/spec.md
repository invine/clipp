# Clipp Relay portal workspace

Status: implemented
Testing seams: confirmed by the user on 2026-10-26

Design decision: variant A, Workspace, selected by the user on 2026-10-26

## Problem Statement

Relay Account owners cannot quickly answer whether their account is permitted to use the relay, how much of their weekly allowance remains, or how live Relay Sessions differ from Login Grants. The current account page gives a full setup guide more prominence than those answers. Repeated values and small labels add reading effort. On a phone, the usage chart initially exposes the oldest weeks while the current week is off-screen.

Administrators must work through a combined account list, general-purpose editor and Quota Plan library. Important actions require horizontal scrolling on phones, and the selected-account editor appears below the account list. Plan identifiers are more prominent than recognizable plan names; unrelated fields appear for every action. The layout makes routine approval harder to review than it needs to be.

The visual system also mixes large display headings, tracked uppercase labels, several small text sizes, a gradient background and repeated bordered, shadowed cards. Owners need a readable working portal that carries Clipp's identity and gives each task an obvious place.

## Solution

Implement the selected Workspace design, variant A, as the production presentation for Clipp Relay. Retain the existing server-rendered stack and relay authorization contracts. The prototype is a design reference, not production code or evidence of live behavior.

Use a labelled navigation area on a quiet green-grey frame, with the work on a white sheet. Separate Your account, Access & security, Account reviews and Quota plans into navigable views. On phones and tablets, fold navigation into a labelled menu; account review becomes a focused detail view with a return to the original list context.

The account view leads with remaining allowance, Recorded Relay Usage, concurrent Relay Sessions and active Login Grants. It places a Connect Clipp action within immediate reach, shows the most recent four weekly totals by default, and keeps setup instructions available without allowing them to dominate every visit. Canonical account restrictions and unavailable observations are explicit.

The administrative view leads with accounts and a visible pending-review finding. It offers server-backed status filtering and email search, recognizable plan labels, a selected-account review area on desktop, and action-specific forms that explain what will change. Quota Plans and owner security controls have their own views. Sign-in, consent, service messages, operation uncertainty and deletion states use the same visual system while retaining their established semantics.

The result is complete when the real handlers, forms, authorization flows and embedded assets deliver this behavior; a static mock or successful screenshot alone is insufficient.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Selected design and scope.** Variant A, Workspace, is the accepted composition. Its account, administration, Quota Plan, Access & security, sign-in, consent, restricted-account and message patterns form one visual system. Variants B and C, the design comparison dock, state selectors, sample identities and the style specimen are reference material; they are not production screens or theme settings. The approved direction is Framed sheet, seed 324836847, retaining Clipp's green and system typeface.

- **Ownership and stack.** Production work belongs to the relay's embedded portal presentation, its authentication/administration HTTP handlers and existing read-view/persistence boundaries where additional query data is required. Retain Go templates, embedded assets and server-rendered forms. Do not copy the prototype's in-memory model, routing or simulated mutations into the service. No frontend framework, external font service, new identity system or new domain model is required. No database schema change is assumed by this scope.

- **Preserved domain invariants.** A Relay Account is distinct from Device Network membership. Email remains verified presentation metadata, not durable account identity. A Portal Session, Login Grant, Relay Access Token and Relay Session remain different concepts. Preserve one-session-per-Peer-ID admission and the accepted retained-session transport policy; the displayed session count is not a count of devices or grant holders. An Active status does not prove connectivity, free capacity or the absence of a Recovery Review Hold. No accepted transport, accounting, deletion or authorization invariant is reopened by this design selection.

- **Shell and navigation.** Use a labelled desktop navigation area of about 224px on the quiet frame, with a 56px context bar and a white work sheet inset 8px from the right and bottom. The outer sheet uses a 16px radius and a subtle edge without a shadow. Show product identity, configured relay host, current area and the authenticated user's email. Render administration destinations only for a currently authorized Administrator Identity; every destination also enforces authority on the server. Your account, Access & security, Account reviews and Quota plans are distinct views, not anchors into one long page. Preserve the current portal root, administrator entry point and existing mutation endpoints. Additional read-only area routes and safe redirects may be introduced without changing native client protocol endpoints.

- **Responsive navigation and review.** At tablet widths, fold the sidebar into labelled menu navigation. On a phone, use a compact top bar and at least 16–20px content margins; the menu opens with keyboard focus management and closes with Escape and an explicit close control. In account lists, keep Review visible beside the identity and state. Desktop selection may continue to use a server-rendered selected-account query and side panel. Explicit selection on a narrow screen displays the detail as the primary view, with a real Back link retaining validated filters and pagination. An automatically selected desktop row must not replace the initial mobile list. Existing selection links remain accepted or redirect safely. No action depends on horizontally scrolling the page.

- **Visual tokens.** Set the frame to #f1f4f2, sheet to #ffffff, primary ink to #192d27, supporting ink to #596760 and relay green to #23654c. Use quiet surface and edge steps, with control boundaries strong enough to recognize. Keep the existing system-font family; use approximately 24px/600 page titles, 16px/600 section headings, 32px/500 leading allowance and 24px secondary figures. Working text and primary form labels are 14px or larger; supporting text is normally 13px and chart ticks are at least 12px. Use sentence case, tabular figures and right-aligned numeric columns. Share a 24px layout gap, an 8px inset-panel radius and a 6px control radius. Phone targets are at least 44px in both practical touch dimensions. Remove decorative gradients, resting shadows, repeated uppercase kickers, large status tiles and redundant value displays. Overlays may have a restrained shadow. Meaning is carried by labels as well as color, with WCAG AA text contrast and visible control/focus states.

- **Allowance hierarchy and mobile refinement.** Lead the account view with remaining allowance, followed by Recorded Relay Usage against the saved effective allowance. Derive remaining allowance as the nonnegative difference between allowance and confirmed recorded usage; retain the full recorded total even above the allowance. Draw a bounded proportional indicator without falsifying the textual overrun. A zero allowance has no undefined percentage and explicitly blocks relay use. Derive the Weekly Quota Window and reset from authoritative service time, not browser local time or prototype constants. Put the week beside or beneath the allowance on phones rather than allowing email and date to wrap into a dense header beside Connect Clipp. Always show units and use binary byte units consistent with existing formatting.

- **Capacity observations and freshness.** Display live Relay Sessions against their configured limit and active Login Grants against their independent limit. Honor each observation's known/unavailable flag independently; zero is a known value and an unavailable value is a labelled dash. If live use is above a newly reduced limit, keep the observed count and show zero available slots rather than a negative capacity or a clamped live count. Limits remain readable when observation fails. Capacity samples and confirmed historical usage have distinct sampling boundaries; do not claim an atomic live snapshot. A refresh is a read-only reload of authoritative data. Show only timestamps or freshness statements supported by the handler; omit an exact sampled time if the service does not expose one. No fabricated latency, growth delta, device list or continuous-connection claim is permitted.

- **Restricted and first-run accounts.** Pending, Suspended and Denied use their existing canonical states and specific explanations. A Recovery Review Hold is an additional restriction, not a fifth account status; show it with precedence over any implication of ready relay use. Preserve available setup help, refresh/status checks and appropriate owner controls under the same authorization rules as today. Zero allowance and exhausted allowance are capacity states distinct from account suspension. A newly registered account can have Retained Quota Usage; first-run copy must not promise a fresh allowance or assume zero consumption.

- **History.** Retain the current Weekly Quota Window plus twelve preceding weeks as absolute Recorded Relay Usage totals. Default to the latest four available weekly observations, in chronological order, with a control for all retained thirteen weeks. The current week is visible at every supported width and explicitly incomplete; do not imply a negative trend merely because its bar is smaller. Use neutral completed bars and relay green for the current bar, a zero baseline, readable binary-unit scale, and honest zero/missing states. On phones the full range must fit through appropriate labels or a list/table alternative rather than requiring the user to discover the current week by sideways scrolling. Provide the same totals as an accessible table, including complete week dates. Preserve accounting arithmetic and history independently of later allowance changes; chart selection never changes underlying totals or their retention.

- **Connection setup.** Connect Clipp opens or navigates to concise setup with the real canonical Relay Discovery Endpoint and a Copy URL action. Keep a compact connection panel near recent history on desktop. Copying announces success only after clipboard success; on refusal, select the readable URL and explain manual copying. Instructions follow the actual Clipp relay settings flow and name each public-client family accurately. Explain that every Clipp app needs its own authorization despite an existing Portal Session. Do not expose credentials in the URL or fabricate an automatic connected state. Do not make an authenticated discovery response in an ordinary browser the success test for setup. Essential instructions remain available without scripting.

- **Owner security.** Separate portal-only logout, Account-wide Revocation and Relay Account Deletion. Move their controls to Access & security without removing any existing owner capability. Confirm Sign out everywhere and deletion with the signed-in account and concrete consequences; avoid an undifferentiated danger menu. Preserve Origin, CSRF, current-session/generation and recent-Google-authentication checks, as applicable. Successful Account-wide Revocation retains the existing logical-invalidation-before-success contract; the presentation must not equate it with completed physical socket closure. Deletion retains its journal proof, pending/completed states and existing status-observation behavior. A deleted account may re-register as Pending with retained current-window consumption; never promise deletion bypasses quota consumption.

- **Administrative list and filtering.** Default to All accounts. Show a clear service-wide pending-review finding and a one-click Needs review filter, with Suspended available as in A. Preserve all four canonical statuses in All accounts and allow review of Denied accounts. Search is a bounded, literal, case-insensitive match on presentation email, applied with status selection before paging over the full eligible dataset. Query text is parameterized and escaped on rendering; wildcard punctuation is treated literally. Carry validated scope through paging, selection, form return and Back links. Reset incompatible cursors when filters change. Retain bounded page limits and a deterministic order; the prototype's incidental account order is not a new global sort contract. Distinguish a matching-result count from a service-wide pending count, and compute each from its stated scope. Empty results expose a clear-filter action. No unbounded browser-side download is introduced.

- **Plan lookup and account detail.** Display recognizable Quota Plan names, not truncated raw plan identifiers as the main identity. Resolve the assigned plan independently of the visible plan-library page, retaining correctness for archived or off-page plans. Effective account allowances come from saved assignments and overrides, not a reconstruction from mutable display selection. Account detail retains status, applied account revision, effective allowance/session values, override semantics, Recorded Relay Usage/history and Recovery Review Hold information. Keep nonsecret allowlist revision available to administrators without making it a headline. Never turn an archived assignment into an unassigned account merely because it cannot receive new assignments.

- **Action-specific administrative forms.** Pending approval presents plan selection, its actual limits, an enumerated reason and Approve account. A plan choice updates a readable assignment preview. Existing suspend, reactivate, deny, return to pending review, credential revocation, replacement-plan assignment, quota overrides and explicit recovery review remain available with state-appropriate controls and the current server policy. Explain destructive or access-closing effects before a named confirmation. Only fields belonging to the chosen action are submitted. Clearing an override and setting it to zero remain distinct; preserve exact byte values when reopening and saving unchanged inputs. No client-side status heuristic replaces server validation. With no available plan, approval is visibly unavailable with a route to plan creation; it cannot submit an empty or archived choice as a valid assignment.

- **Quota Plan maintenance.** Give the plan library its own area with bounded paging, plan name, allowance, Relay Session limit, availability and archive control. Create plan accepts the current validated quantity/unit semantics and reason. Archiving prevents new assignments and leaves existing saved assignments unchanged. Do not add in-place plan editing or client self-selection. Preserve operation identity and proof for plan creation: an ambiguous outcome offers the same operation for retry and cannot silently generate a new operation or reset the user to a fresh creation form.

- **Writes, conflicts and recovery.** All mutations continue through the established authenticated HTTP operations with current revisions, reason codes, Origin/CSRF checks and recent Google authentication where required. Revalidate authoritative administrator allowlist state at request time; prior page visibility grants no authority. A stale account or plan produces a conflict with a safe reload of the affected object and retained list context. Validation failures name the relevant field and retain safe, noncredential inputs for correction. Reauthentication never silently replays a destructive request. Rate limits preserve authoritative retry hints. An uncertain commit is not success, and existing access-closing and reconciliation warnings remain intact. Do not add blind POST retries or let a success toast substitute for confirmed server state.

- **Consent.** Name the verified registered public-client type on the heading and action, such as Clipp desktop, Clipp for Android or Clipp Chrome extension. Present the authenticated account's verified email and configured relay host from trusted view data. Explain relay access within the account's allowance without implying a device binding or Device Network change. Wire Cancel into the existing validated authorization continuation, using the native OAuth issuer's error-response mechanism to return standard access_denied with the original validated state/callback. Validate the Portal Session, Origin, CSRF, account, browser binding, credential generation, expiry and one-use consent before consuming cancellation; issue no code, Login Grant or Relay Access Token. A cancelled, expired, mismatched or already-consumed continuation cannot later authorize. An invalid callback or stale flow gets the existing safe local refusal, never a redirect built directly from query/form input. Preserve registered callback policy, PKCE, consent lifetime, bounded continuation capacity and response headers.

- **Other service messages.** Apply the same compact, task-sized presentation to service setup, expired sign-in, forbidden action, missing page, conflict, rate limiting, service failure, uncertain account result, uncertain plan creation and deletion pending/completed. Pair the explanation with the recovery it actually permits. In particular, a conflict reloads the object rather than offering Google sign-in as an unrelated remedy. Preserve HTTP status, retry headers, the correct safe destination, and all hidden continuation/operation fields that make retries valid. Sparse service messages stay sparse; do not manufacture data, illustrations or large empty cards to resemble a dashboard.

- **Progressive enhancement and delivery.** Serve production CSS, scripts and icons through the existing embedded asset and listener model. Retain content security policy, escaping, no-store behavior for private HTML and the established public/private route isolation. Core account facts, history totals, navigation, forms and recovery links work without JavaScript; enhancement adds copy feedback, menus, previews, dialogs and chart convenience. No-script alternatives must not allow confirmation or authority checks to be bypassed. Loading/refetching retains known content until a new response arrives; errors do not silently replace known values with zeros or stamp them as newly sampled. Use semantic headings, real links/buttons, associated labels, announced results, correct focus restoration and reduced-motion behavior. No decorative entrance animation is required.

## Testing Decisions

- **Accepted primary seam — the production portal HTTP boundary.** Reuse the existing auth server handler and its established request helpers, deterministic local identity-provider double, real migrated disposable PostgreSQL and existing relay/quota collaborators. Drive sign-in, read views, forms, authorization continuations, redirects and failures through this boundary. Assert externally visible response status, safe redirect, accessible facts/actions and committed or unchanged authoritative state. Do not introduce a second UI-only account store or test the prototype as if it were the application.

- **Seam confirmation.** The user confirmed this HTTP boundary and the browser acceptance layer below on 2026-10-26 with “Confirm these test seams.” The visual choice of A remains accepted. This confirmation completes the specification checkpoint; it does not authorize implementation.

- **Prior art.** Extend the existing portal owner-security and administrator approval/immutable-plan integration scenarios, the one-use consent lifecycle and registered-callback-policy scenarios, owner policy-snapshot consistency, current live quota override behavior, deletion/re-registration coverage and profile/accounting history scenarios. Existing template helpers already cover exact byte formatting and zero/maximum history geometry. Reuse those small pure-helper tests only for arithmetic edge cases that are not adequately exercised at HTTP level; avoid a new parallel family of tests for each new template or CSS class.

- **Account data cases.** Cover ordinary usage, unused allowance, explicit zero allowance, exact exhaustion, an overrun, large byte totals, a reduced limit below current observation, current-week boundaries and year rollover, and Retained Quota Usage after re-registration. Verify nonnegative remaining allowance without losing the original recorded amount. Exercise known zero and independently unavailable counts for Relay Sessions and Login Grants. Verify that a Recovery Review Hold overrides a misleading ready indication while retaining the canonical account status.

- **History cases.** Verify the latest four observations by default and all retained thirteen on selection, chronological order, current-week identity, exact accessible table totals, truthful zero and missing states, and unchanged absolute history after a new plan or override. Check that the current week remains available on phones and does not depend on pointer hover or horizontal page scrolling. Retain high-value formatting/geometry edge tests rather than comparing every generated SVG coordinate.

- **Administrative read cases.** Seed enough accounts and plans to cross page boundaries. Search for an account beyond the first page, combine it with a status filter, change scope after paging, select an off-page account, and resolve an assigned plan outside the current plan page or after archival. Verify query punctuation is literal, input is escaped, limits remain bounded, and counts identify their scopes. Check empty lists, empty filters, an absent selected object, no assignable plan and changing data between reads. Assert deterministic traversal without duplicating or omitting stable fixtures.

- **Mutation cases.** Drive each retained status action, recovery-review completion, plan assignment, override clearing/zero/exact-value preservation, plan creation, archive, portal logout, Account-wide Revocation and deletion through the served forms. Check reason, revision, Origin, CSRF, fresh authentication and current allowlist enforcement; verify an ordinary owner cannot GET or POST administrative functions. Ensure unrelated fields are not applied and an unchanged allowance round-trips exactly. Verify redirects restore safe context without trusting an arbitrary return URL. Preserve logical invalidation and ambiguous-result tests already establishing the service contract.

- **Consent cases.** For every registered public-client family, exercise authorize and cancel through real HTTP forms. Assert the displayed account is the current trusted presentation account, not caller-supplied email. Cancellation must issue no credentials, return only through a validated callback with preserved state when appropriate, and make the continuation unusable for later authorization. Include concurrent cancel/authorize, duplicate submissions, cross-account/browser mismatch, expired flow/session, credential-generation change, malformed callback, rejected Origin/CSRF and a service/database refusal. Existing authorization and callback-security behavior must continue to pass.

- **Browser acceptance — the same served application.** Use a browser against a locally built service with deterministic fixture accounts and the real embedded templates/assets, not a screenshot-only HTML recreation. Reuse the HTTP fixture setup and avoid a separate frontend state seam. If a reusable browser launch fixture does not yet exist, add a minimal launcher around that same application boundary; it must not weaken production auth or introduce a public test-login endpoint. Browser checks cover task reachability, responsive layout, semantic names, keyboard order, focus management, clipboard success/refusal, menu/dialog closure, form correction, history range, filter/paging/Back context, and no-script essentials. Keep visual review separate from claims about committed policy or credentials.

- **Visual acceptance.** Capture the built service at 1440×900, 768×1024 and 390×844 for the account, account list/detail, plan library, Access & security, sign-in, consent, restricted states and relevant failure/deletion/retry states. Also check 320px reflow, enlarged text and reduced motion. Compare the selected A references with actual snapshots using real test data, including sparse and populated lists. Check title/heading hierarchy, matched margins, readable supporting text, contrast, proportional figures, 44px phone controls and the absence of page overflow. In particular, require a visible mobile Review action and current-week history, and fix the prototype's dense mobile header. Use the app-screen inspector as a diagnostic gate: zero FAIL findings; document any retained warning by its actual user impact. Do not add fake data or conceal content to make the measurements pass.

- **Verification and evidence.** Run the relay's routine verification profile on the implementation. Exercise the PostgreSQL 17/18 local integration/smoke environment needed by the primary seam and record actual executions separately from skipped integrations; a routine Go pass that skipped database cases is insufficient. Run the existing race profile for modified shared state, especially consent consumption. Record source revision, prerequisites, commands/results, fixture scope and browser widths. The prototype's 36 checks and 39 layout captures are design evidence only. Live Google accounts, OCI resources, cluster changes, native clients, image publication and forced relay transfers are not prerequisites for this portal change and are not implied by local acceptance.
